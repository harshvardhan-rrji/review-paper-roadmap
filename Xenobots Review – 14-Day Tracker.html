How we work

  • 10-minute check-in every day (call or chat): what I finished, what I will do today, where I am stuck.
  • Stuck for more than 1 hour? Tell the group. Asking early saves a day.
  • Write every sentence in your own words and add the citation right away. Never paste text from a paper.
  • Keep everything in the shared folder: Papers, Notes, Drafts, Final. Name files like S2-abilities-harsh-v1.
  • Use AI for explaining hard words and giving feedback, not for writing the paper or finding references. Check your college rule, and verify every source yourself.
  • Show the professor a short outline (Day 7) and a full draft (Day 12) to get feedback early.

Day 1 is set to Mon 19 Oct. If you start on another day, the Day numbers still work; only the dates shown change.
